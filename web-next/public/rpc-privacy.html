EAV7 Public RPC — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 11 August 2026 · Endpoint: https://rpc.eavscan.com · Chain ID 72020

This notice covers the public EAVM JSON-RPC operated for the EAV7 mainnet. Wallet app privacy is documented separately at eavscan.com/privacy.

1. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from RPC traffic.
  • We do not correlate wallet addresses with client IP addresses for marketing or profiling.
  • We do not front-run user transactions using RPC metadata.

2. What we may process

To operate a reliable public endpoint we may process limited technical metadata:

  • IP address — for rate limiting, DDoS protection and abuse prevention.
  • Request metadata — method name, timestamp, status/latency (aggregated operational metrics).
  • Edge/CDN logs — the endpoint is fronted by Cloudflare; Cloudflare may process connection metadata under its own privacy policy.

3. Retention

Operational logs used for security and reliability are retained only as long as needed for those purposes and are purged on a short cycle (typically within 7 days), except where a longer period is required to investigate an active incident.

4. On-chain data

Transactions broadcast through the RPC become public on the EAV7 ledger. That public record is inherent to blockchain networks and is outside the scope of this RPC privacy notice.

5. Contact

Questions: contato@eav7.com · Docs: eavscan.com/docs/sobre · Explorer: eavscan.com

RPC público EAV7 — Aviso de privacidade

Última atualização: 11 de agosto de 2026 · Endpoint: https://rpc.eavscan.com · Chain ID 72020

1. O que não fazemos

  • Não vendemos dados pessoais.
  • Não montamos perfis publicitários a partir do tráfego RPC.
  • Não correlacionamos endereços de carteira com IP para marketing ou profiling.
  • Não fazemos front-running com metadados do RPC.

2. O que podemos processar

  • Endereço IP — rate limit, proteção DDoS e abuso.
  • Metadados de requisição — método, horário, status/latência (métricas operacionais).

3. Retenção

Logs operacionais são mantidos só pelo tempo necessário e purgados em ciclo curto (em geral até 7 dias), salvo investigação de incidente ativo.
